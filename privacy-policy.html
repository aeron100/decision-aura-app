Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

Decision Aura ("the App") is a business management simulation game developed by Aeron Zentner. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The App operates entirely on your device. All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

Data Storage

Game data (such as simulation progress and preferences) is saved locally on your device using standard platform storage. This data stays on your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users, as no data collection occurs.

Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this page with a new effective date.
